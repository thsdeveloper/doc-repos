Every ticket you purchase on Events Manager is saved to your account and accessible at any time from the **Meus Ingressos** page. You can reach it by logging in and navigating to `/meus-ingressos`, or by clicking **Meus ingressos** in your account menu. The page loads all your registrations automatically — no manual refresh needed — and gives you a clear view of each event's status, your payment standing, and the ticket code you'll need for entry.

## Finding your tickets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your account">
    Visit the Events Manager site and sign in. You'll be redirected to your account area. From there, click **Meus ingressos** in the top navigation or go directly to `/meus-ingressos`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your registrations">
    The page lists every event you have registered for, most recent first. Each row shows the event name, your registration status, payment status, the ticket type, and the total amount paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your ticket code">
    Expand or open any confirmed registration to see your unique `ticket_code`. This code is used by the event organizer to check you in at the door. Keep it handy — a screenshot or the email confirmation works great.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the page shows an error, check your internet connection and use the **Tentar novamente** button. Your tickets are stored server-side and are not affected by browser issues.
</Note>

## Registration status meanings

Your registration status reflects the overall state of your booking from the moment you create it to the day you attend.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="pending" icon="clock">
    Your checkout was created but payment has not been confirmed yet. Finish the PIX payment in your banking app before the session expires.
  </Card>
  <Card title="confirmed" icon="circle-check">
    Payment received and verified. Your ticket code is valid and you are registered for the event.
  </Card>
  <Card title="partial_payment" icon="chart-pie">
    You are on a PIX installment plan. At least one installment has been paid, and future installments are due.
  </Card>
  <Card title="payment_overdue" icon="triangle-alert">
    One or more installments are past their due date. You may be blocked from check-in until the overdue amount is settled.
  </Card>
  <Card title="cancelled" icon="circle-x">
    Your registration was cancelled — either by you, automatically after an expired checkout, or by the organizer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="checked_in" icon="door-open">
    You have been scanned in at the event. Enjoy the show!
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Payment status meanings

The payment status is separate from the registration status and reflects the financial state of your purchase.

| Status     | Meaning                                                                                 |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `free`     | The ticket had no cost. No payment was collected.                                       |
| `pending`  | Awaiting payment confirmation from the PIX gateway.                                     |
| `paid`     | Full payment has been received and confirmed.                                           |
| `refunded` | Your payment was refunded. Contact the organizer for details about the refund timeline. |

## Your unique ticket code

Each confirmed registration carries a unique `ticket_code` — a short alphanumeric string generated by the platform (e.g. `TKT-A1B2C3`). This code is:

- **Displayed** on the My Tickets page next to your confirmed registration.
- **Emailed** to the address you provided at checkout.
- **Scanned or entered** by organizer staff at the venue to mark you as checked in.

<Warning>
  Treat your ticket code like a boarding pass. Do not share it publicly — presenting the code at the door grants entry regardless of who holds it.
</Warning>

## Checking payment status via the API

If you are building an integration or want to poll the status of a specific checkout, use the following endpoint:

```http title="Check checkout or registration status"
GET /api/payments/checkout/status?checkout_id={uuid}
# — or —
GET /api/payments/checkout/status?registration_id={uuid}
```

Supply **either** `checkout_id` **or** `registration_id` — not both. The endpoint requires authentication (your session cookie or Bearer token).

```json title="Example response"
{
  "status": "confirmed",
  "registrations": [
    {
      "id": "reg-uuid",
      "status": "confirmed",
      "paymentStatus": "paid",
      "totalAmount": 120.00,
      "ticketCode": "TKT-A1B2C3",
      "event": {
        "id": "event-uuid",
        "title": "Tech Summit 2025",
        "slug": "tech-summit-2025"
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

The top-level `status` field summarises the overall checkout:

| Value       | Meaning                                                           |
|-------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `confirmed` | All registrations in this checkout are confirmed.                 |
| `pending`   | At least one registration is still waiting for payment.          |
| `attention` | One or more registrations need your attention (e.g. overdue).    |
| `cancelled` | All registrations in this checkout have been cancelled.           |

## What to do if your payment is stuck in 'pending'

Payments are processed asynchronously. The platform reconciles pending checkouts automatically on a scheduled basis. If your registration remains `pending` for more than **10 minutes** after completing the PIX payment, try the following steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the PIX was sent">
    Open your banking app and verify that the PIX transfer shows a **Concluído** (completed) status and the correct amount was debited from your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh My Tickets">
    Return to `/meus-ingressos` and reload the page. The automatic reconciliation job runs periodically and may have updated your status since you last checked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Poll the status endpoint">
    Use `GET /api/payments/checkout/status?registration_id={id}` to fetch the current status directly. A `confirmed` response means your ticket is ready even if the UI hasn't refreshed yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the organizer">
    If the status is still `pending` after 30 minutes, reach out to the event organizer with your registration ID and a screenshot of the completed PIX from your banking app. They can manually verify and confirm your registration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Why does my registration show 'cancelled' even though I paid?">
  This can happen if the checkout session expired before the payment was reconciled. The platform's reconciliation job periodically checks for payments that arrived after the initial session window. If your PIX payment has been debited but the registration is cancelled, contact the event organizer with proof of payment so they can manually confirm your registration.
</Accordion>
