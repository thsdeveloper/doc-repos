Repasses (payouts) are how Events Manager sends your ticket revenue to you. After the platform deducts its fee and the payment provider fee, the remaining organizer net balance is disbursed directly to your registered PIX key. You must configure and get your PIX key approved before any payout can be processed.

## PIX key types

Events Manager supports all five official PIX key types:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="CPF" icon="id-card">
    Your individual taxpayer ID. Recommended for solo organizers or individual entrepreneurs (MEI).
  </Card>
  <Card title="CNPJ" icon="building">
    Your company's tax registration number. Use this if you are operating as a legal entity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Phone" icon="phone">
    A mobile number registered as a PIX key with your bank (e.g., `+5521999990000`).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    An email address registered as a PIX key. Must match the key registered with your bank — it does not need to be your Events Manager login email.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Random Key" icon="shuffle">
    A system-generated alphanumeric key (chave aleatória) issued by your bank. Useful if you prefer not to share personal data.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Payout statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `not_configured` | No PIX key has been submitted yet. Payouts are blocked. |
| `pending_review` | Your PIX key has been submitted and is being reviewed by the platform team. |
| `enabled` | Your key has been verified and payouts are active. |
| `blocked` | Payouts have been suspended. Contact support for details. |

## How to set your PIX key

Send a `PATCH` request to `/api/organizer/payout-settings` with your PIX key and its type. You must already be an active organizer.

<ParamField body="payout_pix_key" type="string" required>
  The PIX key value. Minimum 3 characters. Examples: your CPF (`123.456.789-00`), CNPJ, phone number, email, or random key string.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="payout_pix_key_type" type="string" required>
  The category of your PIX key. Must be one of: `CPF`, `CNPJ`, `PHONE`, `EMAIL`, `RANDOM`.
</ParamField>

```json title="PATCH /api/organizer/payout-settings — register a CPF key"
{
  "payout_pix_key": "123.456.789-00",
  "payout_pix_key_type": "CPF"
}
```

```json title="PATCH /api/organizer/payout-settings — register an email key"
{
  "payout_pix_key": "financeiro@minhaempresa.com.br",
  "payout_pix_key_type": "EMAIL"
}
```

A successful response returns HTTP `200` with your updated organizer profile and the message:

```json title="Success response"
{
  "success": true,
  "organizer": { "...": "..." },
  "message": "Dados de repasse enviados para validação pela plataforma."
}
```

Your `payout_status` is immediately set to `pending_review`. You will not receive payouts until the platform team sets it to `enabled`.

## Review process

After you submit a PIX key, the platform team validates it to prevent fraud and ensure the key is correctly linked to the organizer's legal entity. Review typically takes 1–3 business days. You will be notified by email when your payout status changes.

## Minimum payout amount

A platform-defined minimum payout amount applies to all disbursements. If your available balance is below this threshold, the payout will be held until the balance exceeds it. You can view the current minimum in `GET /api/admin/event-configurations` (`minimum_payout` field).

<Warning>
  **Keep your PIX key up to date.** If your PIX key is cancelled, transferred, or deactivated at your bank, payouts will fail. Update your key as soon as it changes by sending another `PATCH /api/organizer/payout-settings` request. Your payout status will return to `pending_review` and the new key will need to be re-verified before disbursements resume.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Use a dedicated business PIX key (CNPJ) rather than a personal CPF key when operating as a company. This simplifies accounting and keeps personal and business finances separate.
</Tip>
