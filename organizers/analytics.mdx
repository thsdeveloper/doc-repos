The analytics dashboard gives you a real-time snapshot of how your events are performing. From the main KPI cards on `/admin/dashboard` to the detailed charts on `/admin/analises`, you can track gross revenue, ticket velocity, check-in rates, and payment method distribution — all filterable by date range or individual event.

## Main KPI dashboard

The `GET /api/admin/dashboard` endpoint powers the top-level metrics view. It returns the following key performance indicators:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total Events" icon="calendar">
    The total number of events you have created across all statuses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Published Events" icon="globe">
    Events currently live on the storefront and open for registrations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upcoming Events" icon="clock">
    Published events with a `start_date` in the future.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Participants" icon="users">
    Total number of confirmed registrations across all your events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tickets Sold" icon="ticket">
    Total quantity of tickets sold (sum of `quantity` across confirmed registrations).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gross Revenue" icon="circle-dollar-sign">
    Total revenue collected from ticket sales before any fee deductions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The dashboard response also includes a `recentEvents` array with per-event summaries (title, status, start date, location, participant count, and tickets sold) so you can see which events are driving the most activity at a glance.

<Note>
  **Gross revenue vs. organizer net:** `grossRevenue` reflects the total amount buyers paid, including any service fees passed to the buyer. Your actual payout — the organizer net — is lower after the platform fee and payment provider fee are deducted. See [Finances](/organizers/finances) for the net breakdown.
</Note>

## Analytics endpoint

For deeper time-series data and event-level breakdowns, use `GET /api/organizer/analytics`:

<ParamField query="start_date" type="string (ISO 8601)">
  Start of the analytics window. Filters registrations and transactions created on or after this date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="end_date" type="string (ISO 8601)">
  End of the analytics window. Filters registrations and transactions created on or before this date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="event_id" type="string (UUID)">
  Scope all analytics data to a single event. When omitted, data is aggregated across all your events.
</ParamField>

```http title="Example — analytics for Q3 2025 scoped to one event"
GET /api/organizer/analytics?start_date=2025-07-01&end_date=2025-09-30&event_id=a1b2c3d4-e5f6-7890-abcd-ef1234567890
```

## Available charts

The analytics dashboard at `/admin/analises` visualizes the data returned by the analytics endpoint:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sales Over Time" icon="chart-line">
    A time-series chart of ticket sales volume and revenue by day or week. Useful for identifying peak sales periods and the impact of marketing campaigns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment Methods" icon="credit-card">
    Breakdown of completed transactions by payment method (PIX, card, boleto, free). Helps you understand buyer preference.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ticket Performance" icon="chart-bar">
    Compares sold vs. available quantity for each ticket type. Spot which tiers are selling fastest and adjust pricing or inventory accordingly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Check-In Heatmap" icon="map-pin">
    Visualizes check-in activity by hour across your event day(s). Helps you staff entry gates appropriately for peak arrival windows.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Filtering analytics by date range

All analytics views support date range filtering:

- Use the date picker in the dashboard UI to select a custom range, or choose a preset like **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **This month**.
- When calling the API directly, pass `start_date` and `end_date` as ISO 8601 strings (e.g., `2025-01-01` or `2025-01-01T00:00:00-03:00`).
- Omitting both dates returns all-time data for your organizer account.

<Tip>
  Combine the event-level filter with a tight date window just before your event to monitor last-minute sales and final check-in projections in near real time.
</Tip>
