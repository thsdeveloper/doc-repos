Events Manager is a full-stack platform that lets you create, publish, and sell tickets for any kind of event — in-person, online, or hybrid — with first-class support for Brazilian audiences. Whether you're an attendee looking for your next experience or an organizer ready to launch your next event, Events Manager gives you everything you need in one place: a public storefront to discover events, a secure PIX checkout with optional installment plans, and a powerful organizer dashboard to manage registrations and track revenue in real time.

## Key capabilities

Events Manager is built around the features that matter most to event creators and their audiences.

**Ticket sales & storefront**
Publish events to a public storefront with rich descriptions, cover images, tags, and category labels. Attendees can find your event and purchase tickets without friction. Each ticket type supports independent pricing, quantities, sale windows, and per-purchase limits.

**PIX checkout & installment plans**
Payments are processed through PIX, Brazil's instant payment network. Organizers can choose to absorb the service fee or pass it to the buyer. For higher-value tickets, you can enable installment plans of up to 12 payments, with a configurable minimum order amount that triggers the installment option.

**Organizer dashboard & analytics**
Your organizer dashboard gives you a live snapshot of your operation: total and published events, upcoming events, participant headcount, tickets sold, and gross revenue. A recent events table lets you drill into each event's performance at a glance.

**Webhook events**
Integrate Events Manager with your own systems using webhook notifications. Get notified when registrations are confirmed, payments change status, or events are updated — so your CRM, email platform, or data warehouse always stays in sync.

**Roles & access control**
Accounts start as attendee by default. Organizer access is granted on request, giving you control over who can publish events on the platform. Admins and super admins have elevated access to moderate content and manage the platform.
