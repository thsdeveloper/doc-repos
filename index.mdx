Events Manager lets organizers create and publish events, sell tickets with PIX checkout, and track revenue and participants — all from a single dashboard. Attendees can browse upcoming events, purchase tickets in seconds, and even pay in installments via PIX.

## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Register at the platform, confirm your email, and sign in to your new account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Become an organizer">
    From your profile, request organizer status. Once approved, you can create and publish events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first event">
    Use the event creation wizard to set dates, location, cover image, and ticket types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start selling tickets">
    Publish your event so attendees can discover it and purchase tickets via PIX checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>
